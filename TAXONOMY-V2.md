# Complaint Triage Taxonomy Revision 2

This taxonomy was rebuilt by reviewing the 20 prepared development complaints as narratives, while treating the original source labels as reference signals rather than ground truth.

## Operational categories

1. **Purchase dispute or unauthorized transaction** — the central problem is a disputed purchase, unauthorized transaction, or misuse of a clearly identified credit card.
2. **Fees or interest** — the central problem is a fee, interest charge, or interest calculation on a clearly identified credit card.
3. **Payment or billing process** — the central problem is payment posting, allocation, processing, returned payment, billing statements, or payment servicing on a clearly identified credit card.
4. **Account closure or cancellation** — the central problem is opening, closing, cancelling, restoring, or maintaining a clearly identified credit-card account.
5. **Credit-report information accuracy** — the central problem is inaccurate or disputed information reported about a clearly identified credit-card account.

## Manual review

Use Manual review when the product is outside credit cards or cannot be identified, when multiple issues are materially central, when the narrative is too sparse or redacted to support a decision, or when the complaint is primarily a legal or policy question rather than a concrete account event.

The classifier should select one dominant category only when the product and event are clear. It should preserve actor attribution, distinguish reports from verified facts, and leave a field empty when the complaint does not state a concrete requested action.

The 20-case mapping and annotations are stored privately. This document defines the taxonomy; it does not claim that the 20-case development sample represents the broader complaint population.
